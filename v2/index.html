HOW TO PLAY

Press SPACE repeatedly to pull the wooden stick toward you. Your opponent will fight back. Pull harder and faster to win!

TAP, DON'T HOLD

Every fresh press adds pulling power. Holding the key does nothing, and your power fades when you slow down.

SURVIVE ATTACKS

A red ! warns of a big counter-pull. Tap even faster to hold your ground.

PUNISH FATIGUE

After attacking, your rival gets tired. That's your moment: go all out!

Drag the stick all the way to your side to win a round. Best of three: win 2 rounds to become champion.
